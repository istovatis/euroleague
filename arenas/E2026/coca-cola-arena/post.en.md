# Arena of the week: Coca-Cola Arena (Dubai)

## Facts to verify
- **No photos:** Wikimedia Commons answered HTTP 429 (rate limit) to every request for over an hour on 2026-11-23, so slide 1 has no exterior photo and the interior slide was dropped. Candidates found: `File:Coca-Cola Arena - 15 Nov 2019 .jpg`, `File:CCA-PBA.jpg` (both CC BY-SA 4.0); add them with `commons_photo.py --get` and re-render if you want them.
- Capacity: the EuroLeague API says **13,221**; Wikipedia and Wikidata say **17,000** (most likely the concert figure). Cards and copy use the EuroLeague number.
- Opened: 6 June 2019 (Wikipedia; Wikidata P571 inception, same date).
- PBA game San Miguel vs Barangay Ginebra on 26 October 2025, part of the PBA's golden-season celebrations (Wikipedia). Slide 3.
- 4,600 LEDs in the façade lighting (Wikipedia). Caption.
- Concerts (Maroon 5, Hans Zimmer and others), Mayweather vs Deji exhibition in November 2022 (Wikipedia). Caption.
- 2024 EuroLeague Next Generation Tournament here, won by ratiopharm Ulm's youth team (Wikipedia). Not used.
- Game time: Dubai vs Partizan, Wed 25 Nov 2026, 16:00 UTC = 18:00 Athens, 20:00 Dubai (EuroLeague API).

**Why this angle:** an arena built in 2019 for concerts, comedy and fight nights, now a EuroLeague home court. The surprise: it has already hosted a Philippine league game.
