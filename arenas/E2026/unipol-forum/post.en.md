# Arena of the week: Unipol Forum (Assago, Milan)

## Facts to verify
- Capacity: the EuroLeague API says **12,657**; Wikidata says 12,700 (and 15,800); Wikipedia says 12,800 seated, 15,800 with standing, and that the 2014 Final Four renovation raised it from 11,500 to 12,700. Cards and copy use the EuroLeague figure.
- Opening: 26 October 1990 (Wikidata P1619). Wikidata inception is 1988; Wikipedia says it was built by Giuseppe and Luca Cabassi "in 1990". We use 26 October 1990.
- Location: Assago, "a small town 3 km outside Milan" (Wikipedia); address Via G. Di Vittorio 6, 20090 Assago (EuroLeague API). Slide 1 and the X post.
- Names: FilaForum 1993–2006, DatchForum 2006–2008, MediolanumForum 2009–2023, Unipol Forum since May 2024 (naming deal to 2027). All Wikipedia. Caption.
- Four tiers of seating (Wikipedia). Slide 2.
- 2014 EuroLeague Final Four and figure skating + short-track speed skating at the 2026 Winter Olympics (Wikipedia). Slide 4 and caption.
- Interior photo: Commons "Mediolanumforumassago.jpg", dated 2013-12-05, shows a EuroLeague game (Turkish Airlines Euroleague floor logo). We don't name the teams; the subtitle only says "A EuroLeague night in Assago · December 2013".
- Exterior photo (Commons "Forum Assago esterno.jpg", 2021) still shows the old "mediolanumforum" sign; only a 960 px thumbnail could be fetched (the original hit Wikimedia rate limits), so slide 1 is a little soft.
- Game time: Armani Olimpia Milan vs Valencia Basket, Thu 5 Nov 2026, 19:30 UTC = 21:30 Athens (EuroLeague API). Milan also host Zalgiris on 12 Nov.

**Why this angle:** the "Milan" arena is not in Milan, it has worn four sponsors' names, and nine months ago it hosted Winter Olympic skating. On Thursday Olimpia host Valencia.
