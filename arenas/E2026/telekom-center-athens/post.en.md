# Arena of the week: Telekom Center Athens (OAKA, Marousi)

## Facts to verify
- **Exterior photo is not on Commons:** Commons was rate-limiting (HTTP 429) and its own OAKA results are all interiors, so slide 1 uses `File:Athens Olympic Indoor Hall - exterior.jpg` (Tilemahos Efthimiadis, Flickr, CC BY-SA 2.0, 3 July 2011), a free-licensed file hosted on English Wikipedia. Same license rules, different host: swap it out if you want Commons only. Cropped (sky trimmed) so the roof sits above the text.
- Capacity: the EuroLeague API says **18,500**; Wikipedia says 19,250 in its intro and "up to 19,443 for basketball" in its construction section; Wikidata says 17,600 (Wikipedia gives 17,600 as the gymnastics layout). Cards and copy use the EuroLeague figure.
- Opening: Wikidata gives inception 1994 and opened (P1619) **1995**; Wikipedia says "completed in 1994". Cards use 1995. Reopened after the Olympic renovation on 10 August 2004 (Wikipedia).
- "Largest indoor venue in Greece" (Wikipedia intro). Slide 1 and caption.
- Eurovision Song Contest 2006, 18 and 20 May 2006, 15,000 seats for each show (Wikipedia). Slide 4 and caption.
- 2007 Final Four (4 and 6 May 2007), won by Panathinaikos at home (Wikipedia). Caption.
- 49-year concession to Panathinaikos, signed 2023; Telekom naming deal announced August 2025 (Wikipedia). Caption.
- Wikipedia also says the 2026 Final Four (22 and 24 May 2026) was played here and **won by Olympiacos against Real Madrid**. The sentence is badly written and I couldn't confirm it, so it is **not used** anywhere. Worth checking: if true, it's a great angle for a later post.
- Wikipedia's external links call the building "Nikos Galis Indoor Hall"; the article text doesn't explain the name, so it is not used.
- The interior photo (Commons, "OACA Arena in March 2024") shows a Panathinaikos home game; slide 2's subtitle says "March 2024" from the file's title.
- Game time: Panathinaikos vs Barcelona, round 11, Fri 13 Nov 2026, 19:15 UTC = 21:15 Athens (EuroLeague API).

**Why this angle:** the biggest indoor room in Greece, painted green by its only tenant. Panathinaikos host Barcelona there on Friday.
