# Round 14 highlight (Nov 24–25, 2026): THE ROAD TEAMS RULED, OLYMPIACOS TOOK THE TOP

## Facts to verify
- None beyond the digest. No photo: a team story, and the player of the round (Moses Wright) has no Commons photo in Milan's jersey (only college, 2019).

**Why this framing:** Seven of the round's ten games were road wins (all six on day 1, plus Partizan 84-71 at Dubai on day 2). The biggest one changed the top: Panathinaikos won 86-83 at leaders Valencia. Olympiacos, level with Valencia at 10-3 before the round, beat Hapoel 104-79 at home (32-13 in the 4th) and are first on their own at 11-3; Valencia and Panathinaikos 10-4. Fenerbahçe came back to win 83-77 at Barça (5th to 4th); Dubai fell from 4th to 5th. Biggest climber: Real Madrid, 9th to 6th (108-93 vs Zalgiris). Fallers: Zalgiris (8th to 10th), Hapoel (10th to 12th), ASVEL (17th to 19th), down 2 each. Player of the round: Moses Wright, 35 PIR (29 pts, 8 reb, 13/16 from two, Milan 95-91 at ASVEL), four ahead of Cory Joseph (31, 27 pts, 5/6 from three).
