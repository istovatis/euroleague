# Round 12 highlight (Nov 17–18, 2026): THE UNDERDOGS BIT

## Facts to verify
- None beyond the digest. Photo: Sasha Vezenkov in Olympiacos' jersey (Zafer, CC BY-SA 4.0, 2026-10-28), reused from day 1's lead post. "Bologna" for Virtus' home game and "Istanbul" for Beşiktaş' are the clubs' cities (the digest gives arena names only).

**Why this framing:** Four teams started the round level at 8-3 (Valencia, Panathinaikos, Olympiacos, Barça). Lower seeds beat four of the top six: #14 Paris won 89-86 at #4 Barça, #16 Virtus beat #2 Panathinaikos 84-79, #19 Milan beat #6 Crvena Zvezda 91-77, #13 Beşiktaş beat #5 Real Madrid 83-79. Valencia (76-64 at Partizan) and Olympiacos (97-95 in OT at Maccabi) won and now sit 1st and 2nd at 9-3. Biggest climber: Fenerbahçe, 7th to 3rd (115-81 vs ASVEL). Biggest fallers: Real Madrid (5th to 8th) and Crvena Zvezda (6th to 9th). Player of the round: Sasha Vezenkov, 41 PIR (37 pts, 9 reb, 6/10 from three), seven ahead of Johannes Thiemann (34, 33 pts, 4/6 from three, in Bayern's 114-117 OT loss at Žalgiris).
