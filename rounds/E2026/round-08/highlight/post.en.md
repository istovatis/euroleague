# Round 8 highlight (Oct 27–28, 2026): PANATHINAIKOS ALONE AT THE TOP

## Facts to verify
- None beyond the digest. Photo: Kendrick Nunn in Panathinaikos' jersey (Zafer, CC BY 4.0, 2025-12-16), a different shot from the day-1 post.

**Why this framing:** After Round 7, Panathinaikos and Fenerbahçe were level at 6-1. Round 8 split them: Panathinaikos came back from 61-60 down after three to beat Milan 80-75; Fenerbahçe lost 86-78 at Valencia. Panathinaikos 7-1, alone at the top; four teams at 6-2 behind them (Valencia, Hapoel, Fenerbahçe, Olympiacos). Players of the round: a tie at 35 PIR, both Hapoel, both in the 118-88 win over Efes: Blakeney (35 pts, 8/9 3PT, 22 min) and Bryant (16 pts, 12 ast, 4 stl). Table: Panathinaikos 1st (=), Valencia 2nd (from 3rd), Hapoel 3rd (from 5th), Fenerbahçe 4th (from 2nd); biggest fall Dubai, 6th to 9th after losing 93-99 to Paris.
