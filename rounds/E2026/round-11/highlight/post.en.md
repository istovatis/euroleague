# Round 11 highlight (Nov 11–13, 2026): FOUR TEAMS AT THE TOP

## Facts to verify
- None beyond the digest. Photo: Justin Robinson in Barça's jersey (basquetcatala, public domain, 2026-09-10), reused from day 3's lead post.

**Why this framing:** Panathinaikos started the round alone at the top at 8-2. They lost 74-76 at home to Barça (57-43 up after three, 17-33 in the 4th quarter). Valencia (102-87 vs Bayern) and Olympiacos (101-72 vs Dubai) won, so four teams now share first place at 8-3: Valencia 1st (from 3rd), Panathinaikos 2nd (from 1st), Olympiacos 3rd (from 5th), Barça 4th (from 7th). Biggest fall: Crvena Zvezda, 2nd to 6th after the 84-105 loss at Real Madrid (Real and Barça climbed 3). Player of the round: Jonas Valančiūnas, 36 PIR (17 pts, 20 reb in 25 min) in Žalgiris' 86-83 win at Milan, three ahead of Talen Horton-Tucker (33, 32 pts, 10 reb, in Fenerbahçe's 109-111 OT loss at Paris).
